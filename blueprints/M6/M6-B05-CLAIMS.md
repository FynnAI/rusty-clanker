| A vanilla hopper clock's item-transfer cadence is approximately 8 ticks. | `HopperBlockEntity.tryMoveItems` (HopperBlockEntity.java:106-131) calls `entity.setCooldown(8)` (:124) after any tick in which the hopper ejected or sucked in at least one item, and `pushItemsTick` (:97-104) decrements that counter once per tick and only attempts a transfer when it has run out — so a continuously-transferring hopper moves one item every 8 ticks.
