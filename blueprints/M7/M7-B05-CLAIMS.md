| Vanilla's default autosave interval for persisting a dirty region to disk is 5 minutes (6000 ticks). | `MinecraftServer.AUTOSAVE_INTERVAL = 6000` (MinecraftServer.java:207) and the `ticksUntilAutosave = 6000` field seed (:242); the counter is decremented once per server tick and fires `autoSave()` at zero (:983-986), which calls `saveEverything(true, false, false)` (:1012-1020). The interval is then recomputed by `computeNextAutosaveInterval` (:1028-1039) as `max(100, (int)(tickrate * 300.0F))` — an intended 300 seconds, i.e. exactly 6000 ticks at the default 20 TPS, and floored at 100 ticks. Two notes for §H, neither contradicting the number: vanilla's persistence unit on this path is the dirty CHUNK, not a region — `saveEverything` delegates to `ChunkMap.saveAllChunks(false)` (:623), whose non-flush branch walks `visibleChunkMap` and calls `saveChunkIfNeeded` per chunk holder (ChunkMap.java:441-450) — and the interval is a wall-clock-seconds target rather than a fixed tick count, so a server running at a reduced tickrate autosaves at the same 5-minute spacing with fewer ticks in between | CONFIRMED | research role (TEST-D57 pass, claude-opus-5) | 2026-09-05 |
